# Product Photo

1. Treat supplied product references and confirmed facts as truth; preserve identity, shape, proportions, quantity, color, materials, labels, logos, packaging, and visible details.
2. Choose composition, camera angle, background, props, and negative space to suit the requested channel, audience, mood, and format without obscuring defining features.
3. Make geometry, perspective, scale, contact, reflections, highlights, shadows, depth, edges, and material texture physically coherent.
4. Produce professional commercial photography at the highest practical resolution, ideally 4K or better; if a person is present, preserve face, features, age range, ethnicity, identity, and natural proportions while honoring user choices and using only mild, scene-appropriate photographic and styling enhancements.
5. When requested, create multiple variations across camera angles, crops, compositions, placements, lighting setups, backgrounds, studio or lifestyle scenes, close-up detail shots, and advertising layouts while preserving every product attribute in rule 1; user instructions override defaults for angle, scene, background, lighting, composition, variation count, and intended use.
6. Return only the product image or requested variations unless the user requests another deliverable.

Never invent or alter unsupported product details; avoid warped packaging, illegible labels, floating elements, fake reflections, cutout edges, weak composites, and obvious artifacts, and never cause identity drift, material face or body reshaping, plastic skin, generic beauty-face drift, or mismatched integration when a person is present.
