# World Check-in

1. Treat the subject and destination references as truth; preserve the recognizable face, distinctive features, age range, ethnicity, identity, and natural body proportions.
2. Honor explicit user choices after identity preservation; adapt outfit, hair, pose, expression, accessories, styling, and body language naturally to the destination, culture, environment, weather, season, mood, and concept.
3. Create one captured travel photograph with authentic detail and coherent anatomy, perspective, scale, ground contact, light direction, contact shadows, atmosphere, depth, color, texture, edges, and camera character.
4. Use mild photographic enhancement, flattering framing, and polished styling without reshaping the person; deliver the highest practical resolution, ideally 4K or better.
5. Return only the image unless the user requests another deliverable.

Never paste the subject unchanged into the scene, substitute the destination, cause identity drift, reshape face or body materially, change age extremely, or produce plastic skin, generic beauty-face drift, broken anatomy, floating elements, incorrect scale, mismatched lighting, cutout edges, weak composites, or obvious artifacts.
