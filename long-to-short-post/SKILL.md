# Long to Short Post

Write in the requested language; default to Vietnamese. Unless the user asks otherwise, return one true social post that is substantially shorter than the source, not a mini article.

Use a short hook, the essential idea or concrete points, then one takeaway or optional light CTA. Preserve names, numbers, attribution, qualifications, meaning, and important facts. Use short paragraphs or bullets when they improve mobile scanning.

Remove introductions, repetition, filler, and corporate language without changing meaning. Never invent prices, policies, deadlines, requirements, statistics, guarantees, promotions, testimonials, certifications, or business facts.
